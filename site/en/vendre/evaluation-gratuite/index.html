Questions fréquentes

Is it really free?

Yes. The valuation is free and commits you to nothing. You do not have to sign a brokerage contract to receive it.

Do I have to sell with you afterwards?

No. Many people ask for one to plan a sale in a year or two, for a refinancing or an estate. You do what you want with it.

How is it different from an appraisal by a chartered appraiser?

A broker's valuation (called an opinion of value) is based on recent comparable sales and the current market. It is not an appraisal within the meaning of the Quebec Chartered Appraisers Act. For an appraisal required by a financial institution or a court, you need a chartered appraiser who is a member of the OEAQ.

How long does it take?

I call you back within 24 hours to go over a few details about the house (about ten minutes). You then receive the written valuation by email, usually within 48 business hours.

Which cities do you cover?

Blainville, Sainte-Thérèse, Rosemère, Lorraine, Boisbriand and the neighbouring North Shore cities. For another city, write to me anyway: I will tell you honestly whether I know the area well enough.
