What the summer numbers say

Prices are rising in the two largest cities. +11% in Blainville and +15% in Sainte-Thérèse for single-family homes, compared with summer 2025.
Supply is rising too. Blainville has 174 houses for sale (+10%) and Sainte-Thérèse 33 (+83%). Buyers have more choice than a year ago.
Each city has its own pace. 28 days on average to sell a house in Sainte-Thérèse, 34 in Blainville, 40 in Rosemère and 43 in Lorraine. There is no such thing as an average city: each area has its own dynamics.
